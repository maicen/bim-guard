### Example - Temperature Expansion of Copper Tube

A copper tube with length _35 m (115 ft)_ is heated from _20 oC (68 oF)_ to _60 oC (140 oF)_ \- a temperature difference of _40 oC (71 oF)_. The linear expansion from the table above is approximately _0.13 m/100m (1.6 in/100ft)_.

The expansion of the tube can be calculated

_dl = (35 m) (0.13 m/100 m) / (100 m)_

_= 0.05 m_

Alternatively in Imperial units:

_dl = (115 ft) (1.6 in/100 ft) / (100 ft)_

_= 1.8 in_
